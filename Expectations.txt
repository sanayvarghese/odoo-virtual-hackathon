Hello and welcome to Odo Hackathon. We are really excited to have some exceptionally talented developers through this hiring hackathon. We are eager to see what you build. This hackathon isn't about coding fast. It's about approaching the chosen problem statement thoughtfully and then designing scalable, well-structured and clear code. This is also a chance for you guys to showcase your development skills and logic because we are always on a lookout for talented developers who can join our team.

So, let us understand what are we looking for. We are looking for someone with solid technical skills, logical thinking, and someone who can work on real-world and dynamic projects. Things that matter to us the most are database design and setup. We really value projects that model data well, design backend APIs, and use local databases like MySQL and PostgreSQL. Instead of relying on backend as a service platform like Firebase, Superbase and MongoDB at least, try to create a project from scratch with minimal use of third-party Peace.

This approach will help you to avoid dependencies that can impact your project when the third party platform has bugs and issues. Use of real-time and dynamic data. Static JSON for quick prototyping is fine, but not for final solution. Robust input validation. User errors must be handled gracefully. For example, if a user enters invalid email, then they must receive a feedback that entered email is invalid. Proper use of Git. One member managing the GitHub repo isn't enough. Version control is a team sport.

Interactive and clean UI. See. User interface is the face of your projects, so try to create clean and interactive UI with consistent color schemes and layout. intuitive navigation, menus that make sense with proper spacing and flow. And when it comes to presenting your project, everyone should participate. Because it's not about who speaks well. It's about shared ownership and teamwork. Using TrendyTech like AI, Blockchain or Chat Boats. That's great if they genuinely add value to your solution.

Understand the tools you use. Don't just copy and paste code. Understand why they are used and what do they do. Here's how we'll be evaluating work, coding standard, logic, modularity, frontend design, performance, scalability, security, usability, debugging skill and most importantly, database design. We'll also be looking at your approach to the problem statement, modular architecture, coding pattern and your attention to detail. Remember, we're here to hire good developers, so try to impress your reviewers with good technical skills.

So go ahead. Choose the problem statement and bring it to life with your amazing ideas. Collaborate, innovate and most importantly, enjoy the process. We can't wait to see what you guys create. Happy Hacking!
